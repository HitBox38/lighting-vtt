# Landing-page artwork

The example screenshots use **The Temple Complex Ruins**, cartography by **Dyson Logos**.

- Artist and map release: https://dysonlogos.blog/2016/07/18/release-the-kraken-the-temple-complex-ruins/
- Original image: https://dysonlogos.blog/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/temple-complex-ruins-production.jpg
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Retrieved: 2026-09-07.
- Adaptation: resized and converted from black ink on white to pale ink on charcoal for the sample map. The screenshots add Lighting VTT's actual lighting, effects, tokens, and interface. Detail screenshots are cropped and all screenshots are compressed to WebP.

Map attribution also appears beside the product showcase. No endorsement by the artist is implied. No AI-generated artwork is included in these assets. Lightling is the repository's existing brand icon, also used for sample tokens.

The sample encounter uses the real GameCanvas, Effect Workshop, lighting, token, and shader components. Arcane Portal and Ember Glow use the checked-in starter definitions. Captures use local data, blocked mutations/actions, and no production scene. Only the development FPS counter is hidden, matching its absence from production. The initial DM and player captures use the same encounter and viewport. Screenshots illustrate a configured encounter; the map is not included in new accounts.
