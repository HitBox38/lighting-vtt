# Lighting VTT

A tabletop scene combines a map, placed objects, and illumination shared with players.

## Language

**Effect**:
An authored, versioned behavior that can be placed on a scene.

**Effect instance**:
One placement of a specific effect version, with its own position, size, orientation, and adjustments.

**Coverage**:
The area an effect reveals through the scene's darkness. A visual glow alone does not imply coverage.

**Baked animation**:
A visual asset whose frames have already been rendered, with their playback timing and placement anchor.
_Avoid_: Light, when referring only to its visible appearance.
